# 00 — Vision

**Working title:** *Bannerfall* (placeholder; not a Stronghold Kingdoms clone — an
original game in the same genre).

## One-line pitch

A deep, slow-burn medieval realm-builder for mobile: grow a village into a fortified
power through a living economy, a sprawling research tree, and tense castle sieges —
the systemic depth of a PC strategy MMO, shaped for check-in-a-few-times-a-day play.

## Why this works on mobile

Stronghold Kingdoms' real genre is the **slow-paced persistent-world economic 4X
MMO**. That genre *thrives* on phones — Travian, Forge of Empires, Rise of Kingdoms,
Lords Mobile — precisely because its core loop is "set things in motion, check back
later." Long build/research/march timers map naturally to mobile session rhythms.
We are working *with* the platform, not against it. The depth comes from *systems
interacting*, not from twitch input or heavy graphics.

## Design pillars

1. **Depth through interacting systems, not menu bloat.** Every number should matter
   because it feeds another system (food → population → labour → production → military).
2. **Meaningful slow time.** Timers are a design feature: they create planning,
   anticipation, and comeback moments — never artificial walls that exist only to sell
   skips.
3. **The siege is the spike.** The economy is the steady hum; periodic castle sieges
   are the dramatic peaks where your preparation pays off (or doesn't).
4. **Respect the player's time and wallet.** Catch-up on offline progress; monetize
   convenience and cosmetics, never raw power that breaks fairness.
5. **Originality.** We borrow the *genre and depth*, not the IP. Our setting, naming,
   art, and specific systems are our own.

## Target player & session shape

- **Who:** strategy fans who enjoy optimization and long-term planning; ex-players of
  city-builder/4X mobile games who want more depth than the typical free-to-play title.
- **Session:** 2–5 short check-ins per day (queue builds/research, collect, redirect
  labour, respond to threats) + occasional longer planning sessions (tech pivots,
  siege prep). A session does something meaningful in 60–120 seconds.

## North star

> "It feels like Stronghold Kingdoms' brain in a game that respects a phone screen
> and a busy schedule — original world, same addictive depth."

## Anti-scope (deliberately deferred)

To reach a fun, provable core, the following are **designed now but not built** until
later milestones (see `04-mvp-roadmap.md`):

- **Live MMO backend / real shared world.** Single-player vs. AI/simulated neighbours
  first; the simulation is built *ready* for an authoritative server (see `03`).
- **Full political layer** (parishes, elected sheriffs, taxes, alliances, leagues).
- **The card/steward boost economy** — designed as a system, not implemented early.
- **Final art & audio.** Prototype uses placeholder/programmer art.
- **Monetization implementation.** Principles documented; no store in the prototype.

The bet: prove that *one village + full economy + deep research + a real siege* is
compelling. If that core is fun for one player vs. AI, the MMO is an amplifier — not a
crutch.
